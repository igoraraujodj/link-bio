About

Manifesto

Design is not the last step of a project. It is how a business decision becomes visible.

I work where brand, communication and technology meet, because that is where the choices show up for whoever is on the other side of the screen.

I do not separate strategy from execution. An idea that does not survive the final file was not a good idea.

I use AI the way I use any other tool: to get to the right questions faster, not to skip the thinking.

Profile

The professional

I am a multidisciplinary designer with more than 11 years of work in branding, visual identity, art direction, marketing and digital products. I work from concept to execution: I build the brand, design the system that holds it up and stay with it until the piece is published. Over the last few years I brought generative AI into the process (image direction, concept exploration and production), keeping art direction judgment at the center.

Creative process

Seven steps,
from brief to optimization

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand the business, the audience and what already exists. Ask before proposing.

  2. 02

    Define

    Turn the brief into a design problem with a scope and a success criterion.

  3. 03

    Concept

    Find the idea that holds up everything that comes after. Reference, territory, concept.

  4. 04

    Design

    Build the system: typography, color, grid, image, behavior.

  5. 05

    Prototype

    Get it out of static. Test it on screen, on the piece, in real use.

  6. 06

    Execute

    Take it all the way: files, applications, publishing, handoff.

  7. 07

    Optimize

    Measure, adjust and document what worked for the next round.

Values

How I work

  • Context before aesthetics

    No visual decision defends itself. It answers a problem or it should not exist.

  • System before piece

    A beautiful piece solves one day. A system solves the whole year.

  • Documented decisions

    Whoever inherits the project needs to understand why it is the way it is, not just how to use it.

  • Execution all the way

    Art direction that never reaches the final file is an opinion, not work.

  • New tool, old judgment

    AI speeds up exploration. It does not replace the judgment of what is any good.
