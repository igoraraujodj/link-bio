Design × Technology

A tool is a means.
What matters is what comes out of it.

I work where creativity, design and technology meet. Below, what I can build with each one. This is not a wall of logos.

  • Figma Design Design systems, component libraries, clickable prototypes and handoff to dev.
  • Framer Web Sites and landing pages that go live without waiting in a development queue.
  • HTML & CSS Code Layout, typography and responsiveness controlled down to the detail. This site is hand-written.
  • JavaScript Code Interface behavior: states, interactions, components and simple integrations.
  • No-code Product Quickly assembling flows, forms and pages to validate before investing in a build.
  • Automation Process Getting repetitive work out of the way: generating pieces, versioning, delivery.
  • Midjourney AI Image direction, concept art and visual exploration at scale, with control over style.
  • ChatGPT & Claude AI Structuring content, copy, research and code prototyping inside the workflow.
  • Runway AI Movement out of a still image: teasers, transitions and video studies.
  • DALL·E AI One-off generation and variation of graphic elements inside a direction already set.
  • Adobe CC Design Illustrator, Photoshop and After Effects for brand, image treatment and motion.
  • Analytics Data GA4 and Clarity to read real behavior and back design decisions with evidence.
