Process

How the project was run

The method applied from brief to delivery. Below go the notes specific to this project: research, references, moodboard and tests.

  1. 01DiscoverUnderstand the business, the audience and what already exists. Ask before proposing.
  2. 02DefineTurn the brief into a design problem with a scope and a success criterion.
  3. 03ConceptFind the idea that holds up everything that comes after. Reference, territory, concept.
  4. 04DesignBuild the system: typography, color, grid, image, behavior.
  5. 05PrototypeGet it out of static. Test it on screen, on the piece, in real use.
  6. 06ExecuteTake it all the way: files, applications, publishing, handoff.
  7. 07OptimizeMeasure, adjust and document what worked for the next round.
